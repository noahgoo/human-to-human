## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative computational intelligence, institutional trust, and high-velocity workflow execution. Built for executive talent acquisition suites, technical sourcers, and high-caliber candidates, the aesthetic merges dark-mode enterprise discipline with luminous analytical precision.

The emotional baseline is calculated confidence: no visual clutter, no decorative distractions, and no unanchored chrome. Every interactive surface conveys deep technical competence through precise borders, controlled micro-luminance, and crisp mathematical density.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Principles
- **Modern Dark Obsidian & Luminescent Accents**: Deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations paired with high-frequency indigo and violet optics to direct eye gaze without overwhelming cognitive bandwidth.
- **Micro-Engineered Craft**: 1px structural strokes, subtle gradient borders, sub-pixel offset indicators, and crisp monoline metrics for deep data clarity.
- **Executive Data Density**: Information-dense dashboard layouts calibrated for scanning complex talent vectors, real-time match scores, and automated pipeline orchestration without visual exhaustion.

## Colors

The system operates strictly on a layered dark chromatic architecture. Luminance denotes operational depth, urgency, and confidence levels.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Canvas & Surface Layering**:
  - `Base Canvas`: `#090D16` (Deep Obsidian). The anchoring floor for all dashboards and core viewports.
  - `Card / Container Tier 1`: `#0F172A` (Obsidian Slate). Primary container fill.
  - `Card / Container Tier 2`: `#1E293B` (Elevated Slate). Floating panels, context menus, and interactive flyouts.
  - `Subtle Inset / Well`: `#060911`. Used for embedded code blocks, prompt inputs, and telemetry logs.

- **Primary & Secondary Brand Vectors**:
  - `Electric Indigo` (`#6366F1`): Primary actions, verified pathways, dynamic focus states, and primary data marks.
  - `Electric Violet` (`#8B5CF6`): Secondary intelligence triggers, synthetic AI generation states, and predictive pipeline analytics.

- **Semantic Functional Palette**:
  - `Precision Emerald` (`#10B981` / `#059669`): AI confidence scores exceeding 90%, verified talent badges, and positive progression deltas.
  - `Credit Amber` (`#F59E0B`): Token usages, platform compute credits, alert warnings, and conditional talent availability.
  - `Signal Crimson` (`#EF4444`): Disqualification triggers, pipeline blockages, and critical system anomalies.

- **Borders & Separators**:
  - `Border Subtle`: `#1E293B` at 80% opacity for default grid partitions.
  - `Border Interactive`: `#334155` for standard card edges and unselected input boundaries.
  - `Border Radiant`: Linear gradient transitioning from `#6366F1` (35%) to `#8B5CF6` (10%) to transparent for elevated cards and active selection states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances structural presence with operational precision:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Provides high-craft modernity and confident authority. Used strictly for platform landing points, core pipeline titles, candidate names, and major metric overviews.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: Built for neutral clarity and sustained legibility during deep candidate screening sessions. Ensures high scan efficiency across variable-length resumes, interview summaries, and analytical notes.
- **Technical & Metric Data (JetBrains Mono)**: Encodes technical competencies, confidence percentages, token burn tallies, timestamp telemetry, and match breakdown factors. Ensures numeric tables and metrics remain vertically aligned.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around an adaptive, fluid 12-column analytical grid tuned for dynamic enterprise operations.

### Grid Rhythm & Viewports
- **Desktop (1440px and above)**: 12-column layout with fixed 260px collapsible intelligence sidebar, `gutter-lg` (1.75rem / 28px), and canvas boundary `margin-lg` (2.5rem / 40px). Max page boundary is clamped at 1720px for ultrawide monitoring.
- **Tablet / Small Display (768px – 1439px)**: 8-column layout with icon-rail sidebar, `gutter` (1.25rem / 20px), and `margin` (1.5rem / 24px). Dual-pane candidate evaluation splits stack vertically under 1024px.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with edge margins of 1rem (16px) and gutters of 0.75rem (12px). All data matrices compress into card-based swipe rails.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing tokens govern purely interior distance and layout cadence:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between status dots and badges; tight icon-to-label alignment.
- `space-sm` (8px): Form input inner paddings, badge clusters, metadata item gaps.
- `space-md` (12px): Standard button group spacing, list item padding, compact card insets.
- `space-lg` (20px): Primary card interior padding, scorecard section dividers.
- `space-xl` (32px): Inter-section flow spacing, structural dashboard widget gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy on obsidian backdrops relies on surface luminance, controlled stroke gradients, and ambient photon dissipation rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Tonal Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (Viewport Ground)**: `#090D16` pure flat matte surface.
2. **Tier 1 (Resting Panel / Card)**: `#0F172A` background with a crisp 1px perimeter border of `#1E293B`.
3. **Tier 2 (Elevated Hover / Flyout)**: `#1E293B` background with a 1px border of `#334155`. Soft ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.7)`.
4. **Tier 3 (Modals / Radial Intelligence Tooltips)**: `#1E293B` backdrop supported by a dual-stroke hierarchy (top edge highlighted at 20% white, remaining edges at `#334155`) with an ambient diffused shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

### Radiant Micro-Luminance
- **AI Processing State**: Active AI intelligence cards utilize a pseudo-border illuminated with a directional linear gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.45) 0%, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15) 50%, rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.2) 100%)`.
- **Match Focus Glow**: Confidence scores ≥95% emit a soft radial underglow: `radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15) 0%, transparent 70%)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on disciplined, low-radius engineering (`roundedness: 1` — base `0.25rem` / 4px). This soft-chamfered profile reinforces enterprise utility and code-adjacent technical rigor.

### Shape Geometry Mapping
- **Action Buttons & Form Fields**: `rounded` (4px / 0.25rem). Retains architectural edge stability.
- **Containers, Cards, & Code Scorecards**: `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem). Distinct container containment without looking bulbous.
- **Modals & Master Drawers**: `rounded-xl` (12px / 0.75rem). Softened window frame.
- **Status Pills, Match Gauges, & Token Chips**: Strict full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast structurally against tabular card grids.
