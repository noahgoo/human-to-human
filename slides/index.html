Known

Hire people who chose you.

A smaller pool of applicants who want to work at your company.

Problem

The people who care get lost.

  • Recruiters sort through millions of resumes written by AI.
  • People mass-apply, so intent is hard to see.
  • Candidates who actually like the company disappear in the pile.

Solution

Limit how many jobs someone can apply to.

Fewer applications means a smaller group of people who want to work for your company.

Tokens

10 tokens each month.

  • Every person gets 10 tokens to spend on applications.
  • A job costs 1–3 tokens, so they choose which companies are worth it.
  • When the tokens are gone, they wait until next month.

Jev

Each applicant is scored for the role.

  • An engineering hiring manager reads the profile, rich media, and GitHub, and weighs building, shipping, and systems work.
  • A data hiring manager reads the same sources, and weighs analysis, modeling, and data work.
  • A resume analyst reads only the resume, and matches skills, experience, and projects to the posting.
  • Name, school, photos, and location are left out.

GitHub

Technical roles get a repo review.

GPT-4.1 mini scores the repository on five things.

  • Data Architecture
  • Performance & Reliability
  • Deployment & Operations
  • Code Quality
  • Team Topology
